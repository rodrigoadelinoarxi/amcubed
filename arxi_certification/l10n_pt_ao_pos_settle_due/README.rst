==============================================
Portugal / Angola - Certified POS Settle Due
==============================================

Blocks customer-due settlement at the certified PT/AO Point of Sale.

Settling a customer's outstanding balance directly from the POS does not
issue a certified document, so this module disables that action (settle
customer due, settle customer invoices and deposit money) whenever the POS
company is Portuguese/Angolan. Certified invoicing remains the only path.
