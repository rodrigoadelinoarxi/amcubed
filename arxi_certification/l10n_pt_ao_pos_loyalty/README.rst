===================================================
Portugal / Angola - Certified POS Loyalty Program
===================================================

Bridge between the certified PT/AO Point of Sale and the Loyalty Program.

This module keeps the certified invoicing flow consistent when loyalty
rewards are applied at the Point of Sale:

- Fixed-amount rewards are booked as a self-paid payment reconciled against
  the certified invoice, so the taxable base is preserved.
- The reward line is turned into an invoice note (it never reduces the
  certified document's taxable amount).
- Loyalty-program products never carry a VAT tax of their own.
- The loyalty payment journal is configurable from the POS settings.
