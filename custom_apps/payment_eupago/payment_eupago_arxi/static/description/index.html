HR National Holidays

National holidays to calendar & time-off


  • National holidays can be imported based on the company country or timezone.

  • Calendar eventes automatically configured upon import.

  • Imported holidays can be pinned to calendar as a full day activity, or shown as from 00:00 to 23:59.

  • Automatic deletion of national holidays from both calendar and time-off, once deletion on time-off is registered.
