Payment Method Fees for Website Sales

This module introduces a dynamic fee calculation feature to Odoo's eCommerce. It allows the configuration of extra fees based on the selected payment method at checkout. The fees are calculated as a percentage of the total order amount and added to the final invoice, providing flexibility in handling transaction costs associated with different payment methods.

Aim

This Module aims to integrate a feature that calculates and displays extra fees based on a percentage associated with specific payment methods in Odoo's eCommerce module.

Use Case

A company wants to pass the PayPal transaction fees directly to the customer, but not charge extra for bank transfers.

A marketplace platform charges service fees only for credit card transactions, while cash-on-delivery remains free of extra charges.



Features
  • Admins can enable or disable additional fees for each payment method.
  • Configurable fee percentage for each payment method.
  • Ability to link a service product to represent the fee in sales orders and invoices.
  • When a payment method with fees is selected, the module automatically adds the extra fees to the total amount in the shopping cart and at checkout.
  • Automatically handles the display and calculation of fees in sale orders and invoices.
  • The fee amount and percentage are displayed dynamically during the checkout process based on the selected payment method.
